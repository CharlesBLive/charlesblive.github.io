About Me

I’m a detail-oriented Software Engineer and IT professional based in North Bend, WA, with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Bellevue College. My background spans enterprise IT systems, software development, and applied AI workflows, allowing me to approach technical problems from both an infrastructure and engineering perspective.

Professionally, I support and administer Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Azure environments, and SCCM deployments in enterprise settings, while delivering reliable end-user support and clear technical documentation. Alongside my IT career, I have hands-on experience developing software and games using Unity and C#, contributing to gameplay systems, UI, animations, AI behavior, physics, and tooling within collaborative development environments.

A significant part of my recent experience involves working directly with AI systems. As an AI Response Reviewer, I evaluated and improved AI-generated programming responses, debugged incorrect or inefficient code, and ensured outputs met real-world software engineering standards. This work strengthened my ability to assess AI reliability, identify edge cases, and understand how large language models integrate into modern development workflows.

I’m particularly interested in roles where software engineering, systems, and AI intersect—whether that’s improving developer tooling, building intelligent applications, or supporting AI-enabled platforms at scale. I value clean code, clear communication, and continuous learning, and I’m motivated by technology’s ability to solve practical problems and create meaningful impact.

Outside of work, I enjoy game development, experimenting with emerging technologies, disc golfing, and following football and hockey. I’m always looking for opportunities to grow technically while contributing to teams building thoughtful, well-engineered solutions.

I’m currently seeking software engineering roles where I can contribute to production systems, grow alongside experienced engineers, and work on technically meaningful problems.
