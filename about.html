About me

I’m a detail-oriented Software Engineer and IT professional based in North Bend, WA, with a B.S. in Computer Science from Bellevue College. My background spans enterprise systems, software development, and applied AI workflows, so I’m comfortable working across both infrastructure and engineering constraints.

In enterprise environments, I support and administer Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Azure, and assist with SCCM deployments—with a strong focus on reliability, documentation, and execution under real-world constraints.

On the engineering side, I build software and interactive systems in Unity / C#, working on gameplay systems, UI, animations, AI behavior, physics, and tooling in collaborative workflows. I treat this work like production engineering: clean structure, debugging discipline, and iteration.

A significant part of my recent experience involves working directly with AI systems. As an AI Response Reviewer, I evaluated AI-generated programming outputs, debugged incorrect or inefficient code, and ensured responses met real-world engineering standards. This strengthened my ability to assess AI reliability, identify edge cases, and understand how LLMs fit into modern dev workflows.

I’m especially interested in roles where software engineering, systems, and AI intersect— whether that’s building intelligent applications, improving developer tooling, or supporting AI-enabled platforms at scale.

I’m currently seeking software engineering roles where I can contribute to production systems, grow alongside experienced engineers, and work on technically meaningful problems.
